Tangents Support

For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Tangents plans your race and helps you stick to it: course splits from a GPX file, heat adjustment, a printable pace band, spectator cards, and a drift check on your watch. It doesn't record runs, so keep using your watch's Workout app.

The app has short instructions in Profile → How to Use. On the watch, How It Works is at the bottom of Splits.

Common questions

  • My race doesn't show on the watch. Open Tangents on your iPhone once with the race in the list. The watch shows your next race, or the most recent one if none is coming up.
  • My races don't show on my other device. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open Tangents on each. Syncing can take a minute or two.
  • The printed pace band is the wrong size. Print at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page".
  • Build Workout says it couldn't read my workout. Try wording like "6 x 800 at 5K pace with 400 jog". Unusual wording needs Apple Intelligence turned on. You can always tap Build by Hand.
  • The drift check says "From your plan" instead of "From your location". The watch couldn't get a good location fix in time. Check the mile on the Crown and carry on, or try again with a clear view of the sky.
