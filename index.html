Tangents Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Tangents is a race-planning app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It has no account, no ads and no server of its own. Your races and workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Your races, workouts and profile

Everything you enter, including races, goals, course files, drift checks, spectators, workouts and your reference race, is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it syncs to your other devices through your own private iCloud storage, under Apple's iCloud terms. The developer can't see it.

To delete it, delete the races and workouts in the app, or delete the app and remove its data in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features people use, Tangents sends anonymous counts to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built for privacy. A count says which feature was used, sometimes with a simple category, for example "a pace band was exported, in miles". Counts never include times, paces, distances, locations, names, race names or anything you type.

TelemetryDeck's software adds technical details to each count: the app version, the device model, the system version, language and region settings, time zone, and display settings such as dark mode. It identifies the device only by an ID that is one-way hashed before it's sent, so the counts can't be linked to you. They're not used for advertising or tracking, and they're never sold.

You can turn this off in Profile → Share Anonymous Usage. The Apple Watch app follows the iPhone's setting.

Location

The iPhone app never asks for your location.

The Apple Watch app asks, only while you're using it, so a drift check can find the course marker you're standing at. Your location is read once for that drift check and then discarded. It is never stored, sent to your iPhone or sent anywhere else.

Weather

When you ask for a race-day forecast, Tangents asks Apple Weather for the forecast at the race's start point, taken from the course file you imported, and at its start time. Apple's privacy policy covers that request.

Workout builder

When you type or dictate a workout, Tangents reads it on your iPhone. For wording it doesn't recognize, it asks Apple's on-device model, which also runs on your iPhone. The text doesn't leave your device. Workouts you send to your watch or schedule go through Apple's Workout app. Tangents doesn't read your health data.

Sharing

Spectator cards and pace bands leave the app only when you tap Share, and only to the people and apps you choose.

Children

Tangents doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
